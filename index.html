I build complex systems that run in production, I build the engineering and science teams that create them, and every now and then I help build a company around them.

My work sits where technology meets biology: bioinformatics, genomics, clinical-grade sequencing pipelines, and AI for bioscience, drug discovery and clinical trials. Today I lead a team of AI engineers in pharma, building enterprise AI and agent systems, including systems validated under GxP.

  • BigDataScript: a scripting language for data pipelines. source
